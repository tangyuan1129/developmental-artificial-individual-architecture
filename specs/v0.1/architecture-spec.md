# v0.1 Architecture Specification

Status: public architecture baseline; not an implementation claim.

The system SHALL expose the domains Individual, Mind, Artificial Physiology, Body, Kernel, Interface, and External World. Each domain SHALL have an owner for its authoritative state. Cross-domain access SHALL use documented capabilities, commands, queries, or events.

The system SHALL preserve the distinction between Individual continuity and software, model, or body identity. It SHALL make lifecycle and data provenance observable.
