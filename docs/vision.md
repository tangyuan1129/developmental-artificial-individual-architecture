# Vision

The project asks whether an artificial individual can develop through long-term embodied experience rather than beginning as a fully formed agent.

The target is a general architecture, not a named character. Particular individuals may emerge from the architecture later, with different histories, bodies, providers, and developmental paths.

The guiding principle is:

> Do not preload the individual. Build the conditions under which an individual can develop.

The scope includes continuity, embodiment, artificial physiology, developmental learning, concept formation, and reliable contracts between domains.
