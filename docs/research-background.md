# Research background

This project sits at the intersection of:

- Developmental Robotics
- Developmental Artificial Intelligence
- Embodied Cognition
- Artificial Life
- Cognitive Architecture
- Sensorimotor Learning
- Homeostatic Reinforcement Learning
- Continual Learning
- Concept Formation
- Grounded Language Learning
- World Model Learning
- Self Model Formation
- Social Learning

It does not claim to invent these fields. The aim is to organize related mechanisms into one long-term Artificial Individual lifecycle and engineering framework.
