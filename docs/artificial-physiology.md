# Artificial Physiology

Artificial Physiology studies functional requirements rather than copying organs. It asks why an organism needs a mechanism and whether an artificial individual needs an equivalent constraint, signal, or regulation loop.

```text
Physical State
      ↓
Body State
      ↓
Interoception
      ↓
Homeostasis
      ↓
Physiological Need
      ↓
Emotion / Motivation
      ↓
Executive Control
      ↓
Action
```

Initial research states include energy, temperature, integrity, load, recovery, sleep pressure, motor strain, sensor health, and safety state.

The intended chain is not `battery_low → sad`. State changes should be represented interoceptively, evaluated as homeostatic deviation, transformed into needs and motivational value, and allowed to influence executive decisions and behavior.
