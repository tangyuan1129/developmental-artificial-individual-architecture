# First Concept Formation Experiment

The first major experiment is a minimal environment containing unfamiliar objects. The individual may observe, orient, approach, touch, act, receive sensory feedback, hear teacher labels, and repeat interactions.

Measurements should ask whether it can:

1. re-identify an encountered object;
2. discriminate different objects;
3. predict sensory consequences of actions;
4. form a stable internal representation;
5. associate multiple modalities;
6. associate a sound symbol with an internal representation;
7. form a first concept;
8. transfer that concept to a new context.

The experiment is successful only when the concept is evidenced by behavior and transfer, not by a generated verbal explanation alone.
