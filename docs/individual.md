# Individual continuity

The Individual owns continuity across time. It is not identical to the software build, model provider, or body.

Planned records include:

```text
Identity · Lifecycle · Birth · Development History · Life Timeline
Continuity · Individual ID · Snapshots · Migration
Relationship History · Personal Experience History
```

Changing an LLM, upgrading software, or changing a body does not automatically create a new individual. A future continuity protocol must define admissible migration, evidence, and rollback rather than relying on session IDs.
