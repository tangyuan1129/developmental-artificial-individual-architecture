# Comparison and scope

Existing work often focuses on one or more of persistent agents, LLM memory, AI companions, cognitive runtimes, embodied agents, developmental robotics, homeostasis, or concept learning.

This project asks how those mechanisms could jointly constitute one Artificial Individual that develops from birth through continuous experience. The distinction is primarily developmental framing, lifecycle boundaries, embodiment, internal regulation, identity continuity, and the attempt to derive increasingly complex cognition from experience rather than starting with a fully formed agent.

This is a scope distinction, not a claim that other projects lack useful mechanisms.
