# Contributing

This project is architecture-first. Contributions should make the long-term research and engineering boundary clearer, testable, and more honest.

## Before proposing a change

Please identify:

1. the domain and data owner;
2. the capability, command, query, and event involved;
3. the contract and failure behavior;
4. the tests or observations that could falsify the design;
5. migration, snapshot, and rollback implications;
6. whether the change is Architecture, Research Goal, Planned, Experimental, or Implemented.

Do not describe unimplemented behavior as available functionality. Avoid binding the architecture to one model vendor, one character, one interface, or one body.

## Documentation changes

Prefer concise, normative language. Distinguish structural priors from semantic knowledge, development data from official life experience, and a provider from the Individual itself.

## Pull requests

Explain the research question, affected contracts, evidence, and unresolved risks. A diagram or small test plan is welcome when it clarifies a boundary.
